Delete Account

If you no longer wish to use My Memory Verse, you may request the deletion of your account and associated community data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to mymemoryverse@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Google Sign-In account.

You may also include any additional information that will help us identify your account.

What Will Be Deleted

After your request has been verified, we will delete the following information associated with your account where applicable:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account associated with the app.
  • Your Community Feed profile.
  • Your community posts.
  • Your likes and other community interactions where technically possible.

What Will Not Be Deleted

The following information may remain because it is stored only on your own device or managed by third-party services:

  • Locally stored favorites and app settings (these can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage).
  • Google Play purchase history and billing records maintained by Google.
  • Aggregated or anonymous analytics that no longer identify you.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or to comply with Google's policies.
