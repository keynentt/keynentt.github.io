🗑️ Account & Data

Delete Your Account

We respect your privacy and your right to control your data. If you no longer wish to use My Memory Verse, you may request deletion of your account and associated community data.

🛡️
Your request is handled carefully.

We'll verify your request before deleting account information to help protect your data.

How to Request Deletion

Follow these simple steps.

📧

Email Us

Send an email to mymemoryverse@gmail.com requesting account deletion.

📝

Use This Subject

Use the subject line Delete My Account to help us process your request faster.

👤

Identify Your Account

Include the email address associated with your Google Sign-In account.

⏳

Processing Time

Once we verify your request, we aim to process account deletion within 30 days. Some information may be retained if required by law or Google's policies.

What Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is completed, we will remove the following information where applicable:

  • ✔ Firebase Authentication account associated with My Memory Verse
  • ✔ Community Feed profile
  • ✔ Community posts you shared
  • ✔ Community likes and interactions where technically possible

What Will Not Be Deleted

Some information cannot be removed by the app because it is stored on your device or managed by Google.

  • ✔ Favorites and settings stored locally on your device (remove by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app)
  • ✔ Google Play purchase history and billing records maintained by Google
  • ✔ Anonymous or aggregated analytics that can no longer identify you

Need Help?

If you have questions about deleting your account or your data, we're happy to help.
