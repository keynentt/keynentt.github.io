Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

My Memory Verse ("the App") is developed by Redeemer Apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

1. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we use Firebase Authentication to authenticate your account.

Depending on your Google account, we may receive:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Unique account identifier

This information is used only to identify your account and provide features that require authentication.

2. Community Feed

If you use the Community Feed, information you choose to share may be stored in Firebase Firestore.

This may include:

  • Bible verse references you share
  • Display name
  • Profile photo (if available)
  • Community reactions such as likes
  • Timestamps

Content you choose to share may be visible to other users of the Community Feed.

3. Local Device Storage

The App stores certain information locally on your device to improve your experience. This may include:

  • Favorite verses
  • Practice progress
  • Settings and preferences
  • Premium purchase status
  • Practice counters
  • Cached application data

This information remains on your device unless you remove the App or clear its data.

4. Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to help understand how features are used and to improve the App.

Analytics information may include:

  • App version
  • Device information
  • General usage statistics
  • Feature interactions
  • Crash and performance information

Analytics data does not include the contents of your personal Bible memorization sessions.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded ads.

Google and its advertising partners may collect certain information to provide and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's privacy practices.

6. In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Redeemer Apps does not receive or store your payment card information. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Authenticate your account
  • Enable Community Feed features
  • Restore premium purchases
  • Improve app performance
  • Measure feature usage
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Prevent abuse of community features

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers used by the App, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Choose not to sign in with Google.
  • Delete locally stored app data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Request deletion of your account and community data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last Updated" date above.
