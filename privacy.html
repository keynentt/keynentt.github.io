Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information My Memory Verse uses and why.

🔑 Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account identifier.

❤️ Community Feed

When you share verses, your display name, profile photo, verse references, timestamps and community interactions are stored in Firebase Firestore.

📱 Local Storage

Your favorites, settings, practice progress, premium status and cached data are stored locally on your device for a better experience.

📊 Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand app usage, diagnose crashes and improve future updates.

📺 Advertising

Google AdMob displays ads, including rewarded ads. Google may collect information as described in its own Privacy Policy.

💳 In-App Purchases

Purchases are securely processed through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card information.

How We Use Information

  • Provide app functionality
  • Authenticate your account
  • Enable Community Feed
  • Restore Premium purchases
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Prevent abuse and spam

Your Choices

  • Use the app without signing in (where supported).
  • Request deletion of your account and community data.
  • Clear local app data from your device.
  • Uninstall the app at any time.
