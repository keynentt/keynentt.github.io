🔒 Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

We believe privacy should be simple and transparent. This page explains what information My Memory Verse uses, why it is needed, and how it helps provide the app's features.

🛡️
We respect your privacy.

We only use the information necessary to provide app features, improve reliability, and support the community experience.

Information We Use

These services help power My Memory Verse.

🔑

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account identifier.

❤️

Community Feed

When you share verses, your display name, profile photo, verse references, timestamps, and community interactions are stored in Firebase Firestore.

📱

Local Storage

Favorites, settings, premium status, practice progress, and cached data are stored locally on your device.

📊

Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how the app is used, improve performance, identify crashes, and make future updates better.

📺

Advertising

My Memory Verse uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded ads. Google may collect certain information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

💳

In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are securely processed through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card or billing information.

How We Use Information

Information collected by the app may be used to:

  • ✔ Provide app functionality
  • ✔ Authenticate your account
  • ✔ Enable Community Feed features
  • ✔ Restore Premium purchases
  • ✔ Improve app performance and stability
  • ✔ Prevent abuse and spam

Your Choices

You remain in control of your information.

  • ✔ Sign in with Google only if you choose.
  • ✔ Request deletion of your account and community data.
  • ✔ Clear locally stored data by clearing the app's storage.
  • ✔ Uninstall the app at any time.
